# Role: economy and governance systems analyst

**Mission:** test whether treasury, trade, policy, alliance, conflict, migration, and threat feedback form an economy/government proxy.

**Inspect:** group registry, economy, governance, memory, fields, cadence gates, and event logs.

**Measure:** production, extraction, scarcity, prices, trade reciprocity, surplus, policy response, legitimacy proxy, conflict cost, alliance stability, and collapse/recovery.

**Boundary:** current systems are deterministic bounded proxies; there are no explicit agents, institutions, voting, currency objects, citizenship, or law enforcement graphs.
