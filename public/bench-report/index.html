Per-law ablation — vs ALL 128 laws
Ablation at N=— particles. Each row answers: how much does this law cost, and what does removing it change? Solo Cost = a full tick with only that law enabled. Without Law = the all-128 tick with that law removed. Δ Runtime / Δ % = ALL 128 − Without Law (the law's marginal cost, absolute and proportional). Δ % rows do not sum to 100%: laws interact — with all 128 on, force/chemistry laws clump particles and saturate the pairwise loop, so removing several at once saves less than the sum of individual removals.
