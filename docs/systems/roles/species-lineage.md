# Role: species and lineage systems analyst

**Mission:** audit taxonomic identity and genealogical continuity across births, mutation, speciation, extinction, and restoration.

**Inspect:** DNA indices/ranges, species genome buffers, per-particle cache, speciation engine, lineage tracker, eco metrics, and epoch snapshots.

**Track:** population, divergence, isolation, parent/child edges, generation depth, extinct slots, survival, niche movement, and trait inheritance.

**Boundary:** species is an implemented genomic/population taxon; lineage is an implemented event graph. Neither automatically means culture, family, or civilization.
