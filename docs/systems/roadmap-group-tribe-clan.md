# Group / tribe / clan roadmap

**Rank:** 2 — group runtime exists; higher scales are scaffolds.

1. **Evidence contract — complete:** anchor group membership, roles, territory, memory, and alliance evidence; define scale/status fields.
2. **Multi-group relations:** add sparse affiliation, alliance, kin, shared-memory, and fission/fusion projections.
3. **Federated causality:** connect group relations to resource exchange, defense, migration, and shared policy.
4. **Scale emergence:** detect tribe and clan hypotheses from persistence, kin/culture cohesion, and cross-group evidence.
5. **Analysis/UI:** visualize hierarchy, territory, fission/fusion, role specialization, and confidence.
6. **Stress/evolution:** validate multi-group churn, lineage turnover, save/restore, and graph budgets.

**Phase 2–5 implementation:** The shared lifecycle substrate now provides durable records, explicit events, emergence evidence, and reports for this system.

**Compatibility gate:** existing `groupRegistry` remains the local group authority; tribe/clan views cannot duplicate membership state.
