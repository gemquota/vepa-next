# Ecology / niches roadmap

**Rank:** 9 — population metrics, niches, food-web inference, and events are implemented.

1. **Evidence contract — complete:** define metric provenance, population/diversity/niche inputs, inferred edges, and confidence.
2. **Event-backed ecology:** correlate inferred food-web edges with confirmed relationship transfers, births, deaths, and environments.
3. **Niche causality:** connect resources, pressure, territory, compatibility, and strategy to niche occupancy.
4. **Coevolution dynamics:** detect arms races, feedback cycles, invasions, resilience, and regime shifts.
5. **Analysis/UI:** show niche maps, food-web confidence, cycles, trajectories, and causal explanations.
6. **Stress/evolution:** validate 100k-scale sampling, deterministic metrics, extinction/recovery, and performance budgets.

**Phase 2–5 implementation:** The shared lifecycle substrate now provides durable records, explicit events, emergence evidence, and reports for this system.

**Compatibility gate:** inferred ecology must label uncertainty and never masquerade as confirmed predation.
