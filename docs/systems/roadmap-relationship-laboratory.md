# Relationship laboratory roadmap

**Rank:** 7 — deterministic templates, batches, trajectories, and clustering are implemented.

1. **Evidence contract — complete:** expose template dimensions, source anchors, deterministic seeds, outcome vectors, and regime status.
2. **Simulation adapter:** run bounded experiment configurations through a controlled solver harness and collect event traces.
3. **Feature causality:** derive attachment, flow, damage, reciprocity, topology, persistence, and lifecycle outcomes.
4. **Regime discovery:** cluster trajectories, detect transitions, and assign evidence-backed interpretations.
5. **Analysis/UI:** compare runs, trajectories, fitness vectors, and reproducible experiments.
6. **Stress/evolution:** explore large batches with worker isolation, deterministic replay, and cacheable results.

**Phase 2–5 implementation:** The shared lifecycle substrate now provides durable records, explicit events, emergence evidence, and reports for this system.

**Compatibility gate:** laboratory observation cannot mutate production physics or promote a regime to a law automatically.
