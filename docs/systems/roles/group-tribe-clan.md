# Role: group, tribe, and clan systems analyst

**Mission:** determine whether a local group is merely a dense cluster or a persistent social organization, and whether multiple groups form a tribe or clan.

**Inspect:** group registry, detection cadence, roles, territory, species membership, memory, alliances, conflicts, construction, and lineage edges.

**Required evidence for tribe/clan:** shared identity across group boundaries, recurrent exchange/defense, cultural memory, and either kin continuity (clan) or broader alliance/identity (tribe).

**Experiments:** group fission/fusion, member turnover, inter-group alliance, kin-biased membership, cultural transmission, and territory shock.
