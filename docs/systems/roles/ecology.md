# Role: ecology and niche systems analyst

**Mission:** observe population-level organization without mistaking inferred analytics for causal laws.

**Inspect:** eco engine, insight engine, world events, speciation, lineage, relationship explorer, and metrics cadence.

**Measure:** biodiversity, niches, population oscillation, food-web confidence, regime recurrence, extinction/recovery, interaction trajectories, and cross-lineage convergence.

**Boundary:** food-web edges are mass/overlap inference; verify against actual predation/material-flow events before interpretation.
