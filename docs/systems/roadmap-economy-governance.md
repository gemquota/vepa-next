# Economy / governance roadmap

**Rank:** 10 — treasury, trade, policy, alliance, conflict, and migration proxies exist.

1. **Evidence contract — complete:** define ledger, policy, relation, event, and cadence anchors.
2. **Event ledgers:** normalize production, consumption, transfer, trade, policy, conflict, and migration events.
3. **Causal networks:** add production dependencies, scarcity feedback, market routes, institutions, and enforcement edges.
4. **Adaptive institutions:** support policy learning, legitimacy, succession, collective action, and economic shocks.
5. **Analysis/UI:** expose flows, policies, inequalities, conflicts, and governance outcomes.
6. **Stress/evolution:** test conservation, deterministic replay, economic collapse, and multi-group scaling.

**Phase 2–5 implementation:** The shared lifecycle substrate now provides durable records, explicit events, emergence evidence, and reports for this system.

**Compatibility gate:** treasury and policy vectors remain existing group fields; ledgers observe before they replace them.
