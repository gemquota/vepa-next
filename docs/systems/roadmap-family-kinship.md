# Family / kinship roadmap

**Rank:** 1 — smallest current implementation. **Current state:** scaffold/inferable.

1. **Evidence contract — complete:** expose lineage and group anchors; distinguish ancestry from social family; add stable read-only foundation metadata.
2. **Kin graph:** add sparse parent, sibling, descendant, care, and recognition edges with bounded history.
3. **Care and household causality:** connect care/resource transfer to survival and offspring outcomes without changing lineage truth.
4. **Family formation and dissolution:** infer households, kin groups, adoption, fission, and loss from persistent evidence.
5. **Analysis/UI:** show kin graphs, care flows, recognition confidence, and generational continuity.
6. **Stress/evolution:** replay family strategies across species, save/restore graphs, and enforce bounded memory/performance.

**Phase 2–5 implementation:** The shared lifecycle substrate now provides durable records, explicit events, emergence evidence, and reports for this system.

**Compatibility gate:** ancestry remains authoritative; family is a projection until Phase 4 evidence thresholds are met.
