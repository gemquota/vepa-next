# Species / lineage roadmap

**Rank:** 12 — broadest current implementation: genome, species slots, speciation, births, deaths, ancestors, and metrics.

1. **Evidence contract — complete:** unify genome, species-slot, birth/death, generation, ancestor, and speciation evidence.
2. **Historical graph:** add bounded event provenance, relationship causes, parent contribution, and species transition edges.
3. **Causal evolution:** connect selection, drift, mutation, migration, environment, and relationship strategies.
4. **Multi-scale continuity:** link lineages to family, groups, culture, synthetic transfer, ecology, and epochs.
5. **Analysis/UI:** expose phylogenies, trait trajectories, extinction, divergence, and strategy inheritance.
6. **Stress/evolution:** validate 64-slot semantics, 100k populations, save/restore, replay, and memory caps.

**Phase 2–5 implementation:** The shared lifecycle substrate now provides durable records, explicit events, emergence evidence, and reports for this system.

**Compatibility gate:** species IDs remain taxonomic slots; lineage history must not redefine species identity implicitly.
